# 19. Business Communication

Skill ID: `workplace.business-communication`

Drafts or reviews email, memoranda, and letters against the book's outlines and formatting conventions (block/modified-block letters, transmittal letters, letters for the record), and flags messages that should not be sent by email at all -- negative, sensitive, or easily-misinterpreted content belongs in a face-to-face conversation or phone call instead.

Source: *Technical Writing for Engineers and Scientists (ISE)*, Chapter 19 -- Business Communication.

## Checklist

- [ ] Have I verified that only the proper people will receive this message?
- [ ] Have I considered whether this message needs to be sent right now?
- [ ] Is this message negative? If so, have I reconsidered whether writing is the right medium at all?
- [ ] Have I proofread it, and would it likely be misinterpreted -- if so, should this be a conversation instead?
- [ ] For letters/memos, have I used the expected format (block/modified-block) and signed or initialed it?
- [ ] For instant messaging, have I followed accepted platform behaviors (no all-caps, appropriate length)?
