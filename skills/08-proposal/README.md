# 8. Proposals

Skill ID: `genres.proposal`

Drafts or reviews a formal or informal proposal, ensuring the problem is defined with enough context and scope to justify the proposed solution, that measurable benefits and credibility are established, and that layout elements (title page, transmittal, attachments) match the audience's formality expectations.

Source: *Technical Writing for Engineers and Scientists (ISE)*, Chapter 8 -- Proposals.

## Checklist

- [ ] Have I thoroughly considered who my audience is, including their expectations and requirements?
- [ ] Do I understand the purpose of writing this proposal?
- [ ] Have I defined the problem in enough detail for readers to understand the context?
- [ ] Have I described the background in enough detail to identify the variables driving my proposed solution?
- [ ] Have I defined the scope, stating how I am limiting this proposal?
- [ ] Have I described what will be measured to judge the project's success?
- [ ] Have I established my (or my organization's) credibility to carry out this proposal?
- [ ] For a formal proposal, have I included a title page and any needed transmittal document or attachments?
