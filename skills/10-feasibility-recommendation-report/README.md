# 10. Feasibility and Recommendation Reports

Skill ID: `genres.feasibility-recommendation-report`

Drafts or reviews a feasibility report (assessing whether options are viable against measurable criteria) or a recommendation report (going further to recommend one option), following Outlines 10.1 and 10.2 and ensuring criteria are explained, weighted, and backed by objective data.

Source: *Technical Writing for Engineers and Scientists (ISE)*, Chapter 10 -- Feasibility and Recommendation Reports.

## Checklist

- [ ] Do I understand what my audience wants, needs, and values?
- [ ] Do I understand how this report will be used -- its purpose?
- [ ] Have I defined the problem with enough detail that the requirements for solving it are clear?
- [ ] Have I selected a manageable number of viable candidate solutions?
- [ ] Have I developed criteria that relate to the problem, explaining why each was selected and its weight?
- [ ] For all criteria, have I collected objective, meaningful data and interpreted it clearly?
- [ ] Have I included visualizations of the alternatives and a conclusion drawn from the interpretations?
- [ ] Have I made a recommendation based on that conclusion (recommendation reports only)?
- [ ] Have I included a contact for more information and documented my sources?
