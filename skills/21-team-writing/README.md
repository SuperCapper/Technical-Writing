# 21. Team Writing

Skill ID: `workplace.team-writing`

Sets up or reviews a team-writing plan against the book's three phases -- requirements, preliminary actions, and document production -- adjusted for whether the team is professional (leader-directed) or student (consensus-based), and checks that a clear theme and a designated decision-maker exist.

Source: *Technical Writing for Engineers and Scientists (ISE)*, Chapter 21 -- Team Writing.

## Checklist

- [ ] Has the audience been understood and the specific requirements of the document defined?
- [ ] Has the type of document, and its style and theme, been specified before drafting begins?
- [ ] Is there a designated leader (professional teams) or an agreed consensus process (student teams)?
- [ ] Have required skills and resources been assigned to teams and individuals?
- [ ] Is there a plan for iterative drafting, followed by editing/revision and final review?
- [ ] Is delivery of the final document clearly assigned to someone?
