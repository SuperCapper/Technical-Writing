# 20. Communications with Future Employers

Skill ID: `workplace.job-application-materials`

Drafts or reviews resumes and cover letters against the book's outline and checklists: resumes led with name/objective/education/experience listed by relevance, and cover letters that show researched, genuine interest and back skill claims with specific examples rather than restating the resume.

Source: *Technical Writing for Engineers and Scientists (ISE)*, Chapter 20 -- Communications with Future Employers.

## Checklist

- [ ] Have I put my name, email, and phone number at the top of my resume?
- [ ] Have I listed education and experience with the most relevant items first?
- [ ] Have I demonstrated researched knowledge of the employer and a genuine reason for wanting this role?
- [ ] Have I summarized my skills and traits using specific personal examples, not restated resume lines?
- [ ] Have I indicated willingness to interview and how I could contribute to the employer's goals?
- [ ] Have I researched the company, prepared for likely technical and personal interview questions, and dressed/prepared appropriately?

## Usage

```bash
python tools/apply_skills.py --skill skills/20-job-application-materials/skill.yaml --input draft.md --output revised.md
```
