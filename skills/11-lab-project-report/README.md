# 11. Laboratory and Project Reports

Skill ID: `genres.lab-project-report`

Drafts or reviews a laboratory report (Outline 11.1) or project report (Outline 11.2), covering purpose, problem, limitations, theory, apparatus, procedure, results, interpretation, conclusion, and recommendation in that order.

Source: *Technical Writing for Engineers and Scientists (ISE)*, Chapter 11 -- Laboratory and Project Reports.

## Checklist

- [ ] Do I understand who my audience is, and does it have specific formatting requirements I should use?
- [ ] Have I clearly defined the purpose, the problem, and the limitations of this report?
- [ ] Have I discussed any theory necessary for the reader, and reviewed relevant prior research?
- [ ] Have I described the apparatus and the procedure used to collect the data?
- [ ] Have I clearly provided the results, separately from my interpretation of them?
- [ ] Have I made proper conclusions from my interpretations, and a recommendation from my conclusions?
- [ ] Have I provided only visuals that relate to my text, referenced before they appear?
